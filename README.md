# Royal Rajputana & Cartier Luxury Wedding Invitations Platform
### 60 Bespoke 3D Wedding Templates, Scratch-to-Reveal Surfaces & Creator Studio

An ultra-luxury, mobile-first digital wedding invitation platform featuring **60 distinct, uniquely styled invitation templates** with **3D card opening animations**, **scratch-to-reveal canvas surfaces for wedding dates and couple photographs**, **cinematic auto-scroll**, and **custom synthesized background wedding melodies**.

Inspired by:
1. **Royal Rajputana Mandap** & Indian Royal Palaces
2. **Cartier Red Velvet Luxury Boxes** (with 3D double doors, untieable satin ribbons, and falling fresh rose petals)

---

### 2. 3D Card Opening & Unboxing Animation Engine
- Real-time CSS 3D perspective transforms (`rotateY(-118deg)` / `rotateY(118deg)`).
- Realistic satin ribbon with knotted bow and golden monogram/wax seal.
- Untie ribbon interaction triggers realistic door swing and falling particle physics.
- Multi-particle system: Falling red rose petals, marigold blossoms, sakura petals, champagne bubbles, and diamond stardust.

### 3. Interactive Scratch-to-Reveal Surfaces (HTML5 Canvas)
- **Scratch Auspicious Date & Muhurat**: Guests rub their finger or mouse over a metallic gold foil, red velvet, or silver shimmer canvas to unveil the auspicious wedding date, day, and live countdown timer!
- **Scratch Bride & Groom Photographs**: Guests scratch an ornate framed surface to reveal the happy couple's portrait photographs!
- Automatic celebratory sparkle bursts once 45% scratched, smoothly fading out remaining overlay.

### 4. Bride & Groom Picture Upload Studio
- Real-time photo pickers in the creator form with instant thumbnail preview.
- High-aesthetic fallback illustrations embedded by default.
- Custom photo persistence and instant live rendering on the invitation stage.

### 5. Cinematic Auto-Scroll Mode
- Floating controls with Play/Pause and Speed options (Slow 0.6x, Normal 1.2x, Fast 2.5x).
- Auto-pauses smoothly when user manually scrolls or touches the screen.
- Real-time progress bar along the top edge.

### 6. Wedding Audio Engine (Zero External Dependencies)
- Real-time Web Audio API synthesizer generating traditional Indian and classical melodies:
  - *Cartier Romantic Strings & Cello*
  - *Royal Shehnai & Tanpura Drone (Raag Yaman)*
  - *Darbar Sitar & Tabla Tarang*
  - *Divine Krishna Bansuri Flute*
  - *Romantic Piano Nocturne*
- Interactive audio sound effects: Crystal chime on unboxing, paper friction sound during scratch, sparkle pop on complete reveal.
- Custom MP3 audio uploader option for couples' favorite tracks.

### 7. Instant Sharing & Export
- Formatted WhatsApp invitation message with deep link.
- One-click standalone HTML download for offline or hosted deployment.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any web browser, or run the included Python server:

```bash
python server.py
```
Then open your browser at:
`http://localhost:8080/index.html`
